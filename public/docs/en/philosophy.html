Design Philosophy

Restraint

Features are cheap. Anyone can add features.

The hard part is saying no. Every feature you cut makes the product clearer. Going from 19 apps to 6 is not regression — it's evolution.

Negative Space

The most important thing on a desktop is the empty space.

Empty space is not "not yet filled." Empty space is design. It gives your eyes and mind room to breathe. A desktop covered in icons and a desktop with only three or four — they say entirely different things.

Invisibility

You don't need to see everything. You only need to see what you need right now.

The command palette is this idea taken to its extreme — normally it doesn't exist. You press a key, it appears. You finish, it vanishes. The best UI is invisible UI.

Data Sovereignty

Your data lives in your browser. Not in the cloud. Not on our servers.

This means we can't help you recover lost data. It also means we can't see your data. It's a tradeoff. We chose your side.

Good Taste

More than three conditional branches? Stop.

It's not that the code is wrong. It's that the design isn't thought through. Good design makes edge cases disappear naturally, instead of writing more branches to handle them.

> A branch that can disappear is always more elegant than a branch that can be written correctly.
