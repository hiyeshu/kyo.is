# Apps

Kyo has six apps.

Not because we can't build more, but because more isn't needed. Every added feature is added noise. A good product isn't a collection of features — it's the result of saying no.

## Bookmarks

Bookmarks shouldn't hide in some browser menu. They should spread out in front of you like cards.

Drag to arrange, group into folders, customize icons. You're not organizing links — you're organizing your attention.

## Chat

Talk to AI. Ask it anything.

It knows your bookmarks and your notes. No context switching — think and ask in the same space.

## Stickies

Sticky notes. On your desktop.

Close the window, they stay. Like real post-its — you write it down, it's there until you don't need it anymore.

## White Noise

A vintage radio. Five channels: rain, ocean, forest, fire, wind.

Not for productivity. It's so your desktop has a little warmth.

## History

A timeline. All your bookmarks and notes, ordered by time.

Including the ones you deleted.

## Settings

Appearance, sound, system.

Three themes: macOS Aqua, Windows XP, Windows 98. Pick your favorite era.
