# Kyo

Kyo is a mirror.

鏡 (kyou) — the Japanese word for "mirror." Your digital space should reflect you, not the world's noise.

Every modern tool — Notion, Figma, ChatGPT — is fundamentally just a URL. You don't need to download anything. You just need a place to put them.

Kyo turns your links into a desktop. Your bookmarks are your operating system.

## Three Layers

**Surface** — What you see. Wallpaper, dock, shortcuts. This is your desktop.

**Portal** — What you trigger. Press `Cmd+K`, everything at your fingertips. This is your command center.

**Depth** — Where you go deep. Chat, notes, white noise. This is your private space.

## What Kyo Is Not

Kyo is not a bookmark manager. Cubox is a warehouse — you throw things in, then retrieve.

Kyo is not a new tab page. iTab shows the world to you — trending, news, weather.

Kyo is a room. You live in it. Only what you choose goes on the desk.

> Cubox asks: Where did you save that link?
> iTab asks: What's happening today?
> Kyo asks: What do you want on your desk?
