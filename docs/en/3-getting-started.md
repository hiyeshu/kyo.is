# Getting Started

Open [kyo.is](https://kyo.is). No signup. No login. No onboarding.

Your data lives in your browser. It never touches a server.

## Add a Link

Press `Cmd+K` to open the command palette, or paste a link directly onto the desktop.

Kyo fetches the title and icon automatically. You can change them.

## Write a Note

Double-click the desktop, or open Stickies from the dock.

Write down what you're thinking right now. No title needed. No category needed.

## There Is No Step Three

Just use it.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Cmd+K` — Command palette. The only entry point.
- `Cmd+V` — Paste to create. Links become bookmarks. Text becomes stickies.
- `Cmd+,` — Settings.

## Data

Everything is stored locally. You can export backups in Settings, and restore from them.

If you clear your browser data, everything disappears. This is a design decision, not a bug. Your data belongs to you.
